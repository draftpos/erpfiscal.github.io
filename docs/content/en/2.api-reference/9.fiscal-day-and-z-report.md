# Fiscal Day & Z-Report

Under Zimbabwe Revenue Authority regulations, every registered fiscal device operates within discrete accounting cycles known as **Fiscal Days**.

Understanding how fiscal days operate is essential for preventing transaction blocks during daily operations.

---

## The 24-Hour Rule

ZIMRA enforces strict timing rules on fiscal devices:

1. **Maximum Duration**: A Fiscal Day **must not remain open for longer than 24 consecutive hours**.
2. **Auto-Opening**: When the first transaction of the day is submitted via [`/api/method/havanozimracloud.api.sendinvoice`](/en/api-reference/send-invoice), the fiscal day is automatically opened. The API response returns the assigned `FiscalDay` number (e.g. `FiscalDay: 24`).
3. **Closing Obligation (Z-Report)**: At the end of each business trading day—or before the 24-hour mark expires—the fiscal day must be closed with an **End-of-Day Fiscal Report** (commonly referred to as a **Z-Report**).
4. **Day Expiration Lockout**: If a fiscal day exceeds 24 hours without being closed, ZIMRA servers will automatically reject new invoices with an error code such as `"Fiscal day expired"` or `"Z-Report required"`.

## Fiscal Day Lifecycle

```
[Start of Shift]
       |
       v
[First Invoice Submitted]  ---> ZIMRA Auto-Opens Fiscal Day (e.g. Day #24)
       |
       v
[Subsequent Transactions] ---> Processed sequentially under Day #24
       |
       v
[End of Business Day]
       |
       v
[Generate Z-Report]       ---> Closes Fiscal Day #24 & generates audit totals
       |
       v
[Device Status: Closed]   ---> Ready for next day's auto-opening
```

---

## What a Z-Report Contains

When a fiscal day is closed, a Z-Report consolidates all transactions processed during that cycle:

- **Fiscal Day Number** (e.g., `Day 24`)
- **Opening and Closing Timestamps**
- **First and Last Fiscal Invoice Numbers**
- **Tax Breakdown by Category**:
  - Total standard-rated sales and 15% VAT collected
  - Total zero-rated sales
  - Total exempt sales
- **Currency Totals**: Separate accumulators for USD, ZiG, and other currencies
- **Credit Note Totals**: Total refunds, reversals, and adjusted VAT
- **Cryptographic Audit Signature**: Permanent non-resettable fiscal memory entry

## Best Practices for ERP & POS Integrations

1. **Automate End-of-Day (EOD)**: Configure your POS or ERP system to trigger an End-of-Day routine at closing time (e.g., midnight or end of cashier shift).
2. **Shift Reconciliation**: Compare your internal POS tender totals (Cash, Card, EcoCash, InnBucks) against the Z-Report tax and sales totals before closing the shift.
3. **Emergency Auto-Close**: If your business operates 24/7 (such as fuel stations or convenience stores), schedule a job to close the day before 23 hours and 50 minutes of continuous operation to avoid ZIMRA lockout.
