# Go-Live & Compliance Checklist

Before switching your ERP, Point of Sale, or custom invoicing solution from a testing environment to live production with ZIMRA, complete this pre-flight verification checklist.

---

## 1. Statutory & Account Registration

- [ ] **ZIMRA Registration**: Confirm your entity is registered on the ZIMRA e-Tafs portal and your Business Partner (BP/TIN) and VAT numbers are active.
- [ ] **Approved Fiscal Device Allocation**: Verify that your physical fiscal device or virtual fiscalizer serial number (`device_sn`) has been approved and issued by ZIMRA or an authorized provider.
- [ ] **HavanoZimra Cloud Profile**: Ensure your user profile and device serial numbers are mapped and enabled in the HavanoZimra Cloud dashboard.
- [ ] **Production API Credentials**: Generate your live `API_KEY` and `API_SECRET` from HavanoZimra Cloud and store them securely in your production environment variables (e.g. `.env` or AWS Secrets Manager).

---

## 2. API Integration & Operational Testing

- [ ] **Ping Connectivity Check**: Successfully run [`pingzimra`](/en/api-reference/ping-zimra) from your production server or till terminal and verify that `ResponseCode` returns `"0"`.
- [ ] **CSRF Token Lifecycle**: Ensure your application automatically fetches and caches the CSRF token via [`token`](/en/api-reference/token), passing `X-Frappe-CSRF-Token` in all subsequent requests.
- [ ] **Token Expiration Recovery**: Confirm that your HTTP client catches HTTP 403 or CSRF expiration responses, refreshes the token, and retries the request seamlessly.
- [ ] **Unique Invoice Sequencing**: Test that your internal invoice numbers (`invoice_number`) are globally unique and cannot be submitted twice to prevent duplicate document errors.

---

## 3. Tax Calculations & XML Line Items

- [ ] **Mandatory XML Fields**: Confirm that all 10 required XML elements (`<HH>`, `<ITEMCODE>`, `<ITEMNAME>`, `<ITEMNAME2>`, `<QTY>`, `<PRICE>`, `<TOTAL>`, `<VAT>`, `<VATR>`, `<VNAME>`) are populated in every line item.
- [ ] **Accurate Tax Category Mapping**:
  - Standard goods are tagged with `<VNAME>VAT</VNAME>` and `<VATR>0.15</VATR>`.
  - Zero-rated foodstuffs/exports are tagged with `<VNAME>ZERO RATED</VNAME>` and `<VATR>0.00</VATR>`.
  - Exempt goods/services are tagged with `<VNAME>EXEMPT</VNAME>` and `<VATR>0.00</VATR>`.
- [ ] **Arithmetic Verification**: Confirm that $\text{TOTAL} = \text{QTY} \times \text{PRICE}$ and the line item `<VAT>` equals the exact tax portion rounded to 2 decimal places.
- [ ] **XML Special Character Escaping**: Ensure that product names containing ampersands (`&`), quotes (`"`), or brackets (`<`, `>`) are properly XML-escaped (`&amp;`, `&quot;`, `&lt;`).

---

## 4. Multi-Currency & Tender Handling

- [ ] **Currency Parameter**: Ensure `currency` parameter is strictly set to `USD`, `ZIG`, or other authorized codes, matching the currency in `items_xml`.
- [ ] **RBZ Exchange Rates**: For dual-currency tenders or price conversions, verify that the prevailing RBZ interbank rate is recorded on the transaction.

---

## 5. Credit Notes & Reversals

- [ ] **Original Invoice Reference**: Verify that credit notes set `invoice_flag = 1` and provide both `original_invoice_no` and `global_invoice_no`.
- [ ] **Reason / Comment**: Ensure a mandatory descriptive note is passed in `invoice_comment` detailing the return or adjustment.
- [ ] **Database Persistence**: Ensure your database stores the `GlobalInvoiceNo` returned by ZIMRA for every sales invoice so it can be referenced if a return occurs.

---

## 6. Offline Mode & Business Continuity

- [ ] **Local Queue Storage**: Implement a local SQLite, Postgres, or SQL Server table to buffer transactions when ZIMRA servers are unreachable.
- [ ] **Background Sync Service**: Verify that your sync worker periodically calls [`pingzimra`](/en/api-reference/ping-zimra) and pushes queued offline invoices in strict chronological order once connectivity is restored.
- [ ] **Cashier Fallback**: Train POS operators to continue issuing receipts in offline mode without halting checkout lanes during network drops.

---

## 7. Receipt Printing & QR Code Compliance

- [ ] **Mandatory Receipt Layout**: Test thermal (80mm) or A4 printouts to confirm taxpayer name, TIN, VAT number, fiscal day, and `GlobalInvoiceNo` are printed.
- [ ] **Scannable ZIMRA QR Code**: Ensure the printed QR code encodes the returned `VerificationURL` and scans cleanly using the ZIMRA mobile verification app or smartphone camera.
- [ ] **Fiscal Day Management**: Schedule your End-of-Day (Z-Report) procedure so that no fiscal day exceeds the 24-hour limit.
