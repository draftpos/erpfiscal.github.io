# Multi-Currency & ZiG Compliance

Under Zimbabwe's multi-currency regime and Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe (RBZ) monetary guidelines, businesses routinely transact in both **US Dollars (USD)** and **Zimbabwe Gold (ZiG)**, as well as other designated foreign currencies.

HavanoZimra Cloud supports compliant multi-currency fiscalization, ensuring that tax obligations, exchange rates, and line-item taxes are recorded accurately with ZIMRA.

---

## Supported Currency Codes

When populating the `currency` parameter in [`/api/method/havanozimracloud.api.sendinvoice`](/en/api-reference/send-invoice), use standard ISO or ZIMRA-recognized three-letter currency symbols:

| Currency Code | Currency Name | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `USD` | United States Dollar | Most widely used settlement currency for commercial retail and B2B invoices. |
| `ZIG` | Zimbabwe Gold | Official national currency of Zimbabwe introduced in April 2024. |
| `ZAR` | South African Rand | Accepted for regional trade and border commerce. |
| `GBP` | British Pound Sterling | Foreign currency settlement. |
| `EUR` | Euro | Foreign currency settlement. |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The currency specified in the `currency` parameter applies to the **entire invoice**, including all unit prices (`PRICE`), line totals (`TOTAL`), and VAT amounts (`VAT`) inside `items_xml`. Do not mix multiple currencies within the same invoice payload.

---

## Split Payments / Dual Currency Transactions

If a customer tenders payment using a combination of currencies (for example, part payment in USD cash and the remainder via ZiG POS card swipe):

1. **Primary Currency Principle**: Under ZIMRA rules, the fiscal tax invoice must be issued in the **currency in which the sale is primarily denominated** (e.g., `USD` if goods are priced in USD).
2. **Tender Breakdown on Printed Receipt**:
   On the physical customer receipt or PDF, you may display a breakdown of the tender amounts:
   ```text
   TOTAL DUE:                             USD 20.00
   ------------------------------------------------
   TENDER BREAKDOWN:
     USD Cash:                             USD 10.00
     ZiG Card Swipe (Interbank Rate 26.5): ZiG 265.00
     Equivalent USD:                       USD 10.00
   ------------------------------------------------
   CHANGE:                                 USD  0.00
   ```
3. **Tax Remittance**: ZIMRA requires VAT collected in foreign currency (USD) to be remitted to ZIMRA in that same foreign currency. The fiscal data transmitted to HavanoZimra Cloud preserves this currency breakdown for statutory tax returns.

---

## Exchange Rate Requirements

When converting prices between USD and ZiG:
- Always use the official **willing-buyer willing-seller interbank exchange rate** published daily by the Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe (RBZ).
- Record the prevailing exchange rate on the printed receipt for audit compliance.
- Ensure that rounding on converted amounts does not cause a discrepancy between line items and invoice totals.
